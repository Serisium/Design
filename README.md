# Plugins

## [Sanzō Wada Color Library](figma/color)
Explore color combinations from Sanzō Wada's book _A Dictionary of Color Combinations_ directly in Figma. Apply colors right into your canvas, with different selections and lightness values per role.

- Scan for unassigned colors and apply bring them into your color palette
- Switch colors inside of a palette and see your design change immediately
- Choose between different lightness values your palette, or leave them in their original tone.
